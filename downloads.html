Get the app

Open OminiTrain

OminiTrain runs directly from its official link on phones, tablets and computers. No file download is needed.

Open OminiTrain Show the QR code

On Android

  1. Open station-light.lovable.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) and choose Add to Home screen.
  3. OminiTrain then opens like an installed app.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the app link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. OminiTrain then opens full screen from your home screen.

On a computer

Open the app link in any current browser. The high-contrast layout adapts to the window size.
