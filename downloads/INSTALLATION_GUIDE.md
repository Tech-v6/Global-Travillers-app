# Installing OminiTrain App

**OminiTrain App — Your Journey, Everywhere.**

OminiTrain opens directly at https://station-light.lovable.app. There is no file to download.

## Android

1. Open the app link in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu (⋮) and choose **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open OminiTrain from your home screen; it runs full screen.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open the app link in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open OminiTrain from your home screen.

## Computer

Open the app link in any current browser. The high-contrast layout adapts to the window size.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — needed only for journey reminders.
- **Location** — needed only if you ask OminiTrain to plan from where you are.

Both are optional and requested only when the feature is used.

## Notes

Installer files previously referenced here are no longer available; do not rely on an unavailable download. Travel information needs a network connection — without one, OminiTrain says so rather than showing old times as current.
