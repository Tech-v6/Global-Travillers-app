OminiTrain App
Your Journey, Everywhere.

OminiTrain is a travel companion for people on the move — planning a journey, travelling it, and keeping it afterwards. It brings journey planning, travel information, stations and destinations, active journeys, reminders, travel assistance and OminiJourney Memory into one app.

Open OminiTrain How to get the app

Before · During · After

Support across the whole journey

Before you travel

Plan a journey between places, look up stations and destinations, and set a reminder so departure time does not slip past you.

While you travel

Follow your active journey, see travel information for your route and stops, and get assistance when a connection or an exit needs a decision.

After you arrive

Keep the journey in OminiJourney Memory — the route you actually travelled, with your own title, notes and photos.

What the app does

One app, one journey

Journey planning

Plan from and to a place, including addresses and stations, and review the legs of the journey.

Travel information

Departure and arrival information for stations and stops, with the source and how recently it was checked.

Stations & destinations

Explore countries, cities, stations and destination information, and open a station board or map view.

Active journeys

One running journey the app keeps track of, from departure through to arrival.

Reminders

A journey reminder that arrives at the right moment, using your device's own notification system.

Travel assistance

Guidance for what to do next, connection problems and getting off at the right stop.

OminiJourney Memory

Completed journeys you can title, describe, print, save as a document or share.

Accessibility

High-contrast display, larger text support, calm mode and spoken announcements.

About

An independent project

OminiTrain App is an independent global travel-tech project. It is not owned by, affiliated with or endorsed by any railway, transport authority or operator. Travel information comes from public transport data sources and is shown with its source.

How OminiTrain talks about information. Live information is labelled live only when it is genuinely confirmed live. Timetable information is labelled as scheduled. When no reliable source is available, OminiTrain says the information is unavailable rather than guessing.
