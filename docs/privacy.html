Privacy

Privacy information

OminiTrain App is an independent global travel-tech project. This page explains, in plain terms, how the app and this website handle information.

What stays on your device

Your planned and saved journeys, your language and display settings, reminders and your OminiJourney Memory entries — including titles, notes and photos you add — are kept on your device unless you choose to save or share them elsewhere.

Location

Location is used only if you ask OminiTrain to plan from where you are, and only after you grant permission. Addresses and places you type are not treated as your location.

Notifications

Notifications are used for journey reminders. Sound and vibration depend on what your device allows for OminiTrain.

Contacts, sharing, printing

When you share a journey or choose a contact, OminiTrain uses your device's own sharing, email, printing and contact features. Nothing is sent without your action, and your address book is not uploaded.

Travel data sources

Journey and departure information comes from public transport data sources, shown with the source and how recently it was checked. Live information is labelled live only when it is genuinely confirmed live.

This website

This site is published with GitHub Pages and has no accounts or tracking of its own. The QR code image on the QR page is produced by goQR.me (api.qrserver.com) when that page loads.
